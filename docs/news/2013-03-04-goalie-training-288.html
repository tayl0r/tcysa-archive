Goalie Training

Last updated 2013-03-04

Free Goalie Training

Who:     This goalie training is open to any player U8-U19 who has been rostered on a team between fall 2012 and present.  Players may attend one or both sessions.  

When:    Friday February 22, and Friday March 1

Time:      6:30 pm to 8:00 pm

Where:   RAC Turf field
                8345 Steilacoom Road SE (Corner of Steilacoom and Marvin Road)

RSVP:   [email removed] – please RSVP if you plan to attend

Bring:    Keepers need to bring: gloves, shin guards, warm clothes (long sleeve tops and pants), warm jacket, and water.

Week one: Revisiting the basics.  Working on footwork, stance, positioning, and proper keeper focused warm up.  Progress onto fundamentals of making the save as well as distribution.  Some conditioning will be included.

Week two: Review from week one. More shot stopping, diving, conditioning and games. 

Instructor  Sammy Havens

-Holds National and Advanced National Diplomas from the NSCAA.
