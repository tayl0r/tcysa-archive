TC United

Fridays with Fred! - Spring 2016

 TC United is happy to offer free technical training for current select and recreational players aged U12 and below seeking to increase their ball mastery skills in the sport they love.  TC United Director of Coaching Fred Thompson will work directly with players to help develop their individual skills and techniques to build more confidence on the ball. This is a great way to get players excited about training, and we will teach drills that they can take home and repeat on the playground, in the backyard, or even inside the house (sorry parents)!

Bring a soccer ball and cleats

Players U12 and younger
Location: Regional Athletic Complex (RAC) Turf Field #1
Cost: FREE (Must be registered through a TCYSA affiliated program)*
Dates/Times: Friday's 5:30-6:45pm

Continuing every Friday unless posted otherwise on our Facebook or Website.
