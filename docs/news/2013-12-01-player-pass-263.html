Player Pass
District 7 operates a Player Pass, for development purposes, enabling players to guest on teams playing in the higher recreational divisions (Green). The Pass may be used by other District 7 club recreational players to guest on a team in an age appropriate Green Division. Direct your questions to your association representative on the District Board. 
 
 The Pass may be used by players playing in the Orange or Blue Divisions to guest on teams in the Green division but not vice-versa, (on the basis that it is for players seeking more competitive experience). 

You can find the player pass rules in the download section of this website. Direct your questions to your club/association representative or to [name removed], TCYSA Manager at [email removed]
