BLACKHILLS FC UNDER THE LIGHTS

The "Under the Lights" is a supplemental field player and goalkeeping program that provides an opportunity for children to play, learn and enjoy playing soccer. The Blackhills FC Coaching Staff will lead the instruction assisted by Blackhills FC players. All Coaches, regardless of their experience level, are welcome to participate for coach’s education.
