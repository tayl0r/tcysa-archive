Blackhills FC Under the Lights

The "Under the Lights" is a supplemental field player and goalkeeping program that provides an opportunity for children to play, learn and enjoy playing soccer. 

The Blackhills FC Coaching Staff will lead the instruction assisted by Blackhills FC players. 

Children aged 6 to 16 will be grouped by a combination of age, ability level and friend(s)/teammates.

Dates: January 24, 31, February 7 and 21

Location: Tumwater High School

Cost: $45

Program Registration: limited enrollment, www.blackhillsfc.org (login top right hand corner)
