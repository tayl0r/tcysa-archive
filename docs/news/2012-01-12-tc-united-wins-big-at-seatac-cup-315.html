TC United Wins Big at SeaTac Cup

Last updated 2012-01-12

GU18 TC United coached by Charles Paulson were Champions at the SeaTac Cup.

GU14 TC United coached by Crowe were also Champions at the SeaTac Cup.

Way to go TC United.
