Looking for soccer balls

Last updated 2013-07-29

Seeking donation of soccer balls
Our organization has partnered with a local church on a project in Fiji this coming August. The project is at a center for women who have been rescued from human trafficking and are being retrained in many areas. There are many kids that live on the Suva, Fiji campus and I am wondering if among your Youth Footbal community we would be able to acquire new or gently used futbols to take overseas for these children lacking such resources.
We are looking for about 10 - 12 balls and perhaps some used team uniforms of youth sizes or small adult.
 
Larry Dominguez, Founding President
