Silent Saturday Spring 2016

May 7th

For Teams U10 to U15

This a time for parents to encourage player communication on the field by observing Silent Sideline behavior during the game.

From  "Whistle to Whistle" Parents, Coaches and Sidelines are being asked to observe Silent Saturday.

Silent Saturday does not apply to the referee.

Coaches should communicate with their players before the game, during half time and after the game.
