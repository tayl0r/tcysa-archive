TCYSA Manager Job Announcement

Last updated 2017-04-25

TCYSA Club Manager Position(Part Time employment):

 

The Thurston County Youth Soccer Association is seeking qualified applicants for the Club Manager position.  See the attached job description.  Employment is anticipated to commence on May 1, 2017.  The position is part time(approximately 1000 hours per year) and compensation of approximately $15,000 per year is anticipated, depending upon experience.  Applicants should provide a letter of interest and/or resume to treasurer@thurstoncountysoccer.com. 

 

All applications must be submitted electronically by April 20, 2017.
