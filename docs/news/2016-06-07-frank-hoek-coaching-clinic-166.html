Last updated 2016-06-07

 

Frank Hoek Coaching Clinic

Blackhills FC is hosting Frans Hoek, Master Coach and Teacher for Clubs and National teams such as Manchester United and the Netherlands Mens World Cup 2014  Assistant Coach, for a coach development workshop to Develop Team Coaches in Coaching Goalkeepers within the team during training and games.  Frans is a world leader in Coach Development and also has coaching experience at the highest levels with FC Bayern Munich, FC Barcelona, Ajax and the Polish National Team.

June 3,4,5

Course Registration:  Email [name removed] <[email removed]>

Field and Classroom Location:  TBD, Olympia

Course Fee:  $200 (Limited Enrollment)

Aim of the Course :

Develop Team Coaches in Coaching goalkeepers within the team during training and games.

Focus :

Role of the goalkeeper and theory / field session on :

They Phase 2-3 (coaching team, positioning and defending the space behind the defense)

They Phase 4 (positioning, role of  GK in shots, crosses, through balls and 1v1)

We Phase 1-2 and going back to Phase 1 in own half -  build up from goal kick / pass back – keeping possession and also vs full pressure skipping lines to find midfield or forward players and playing from there or from second ball.

Course Outline : 

June 3  – Friday – 7-:30pm -10:00 PM

June 4 – Saturday – 9am -12pm and 1:30pm-6:30 PM

June 5  – Sunday – 8:00am-10:00 AM

12 hour workshop
