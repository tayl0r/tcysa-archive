Up and Coming U23 Team South Sound FC  Lakewood, WA

Games and Training Opportunity

http://www.southsoundfc.com/

Your team game day schedule would look something like this:

·  1.5 hr Top Level training session with South Sound Players and Coaches (start 2.5 hrs before game)- $30 per player and includes a free dri-fit SSFC tee shirt

·  1 hour break- Youth players can eat, watch our first team warm up, hang out on the field and listen to the coaches, etc

·  Game time- Join our team for pre-game locker room talk, walk out with the team, be the ball-kid for the game, and play a scrimmage during halftime.
