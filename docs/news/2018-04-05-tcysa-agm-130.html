TCYSA AGM

Last updated 2018-04-05

TCYSA Annual General Meeting 

The TCYSA AGM will be held Sunday, May 6, at 6:30. The meeting will take place at Lacey Fire Station #31 off of Franz Road.

There are several positions that are up for election. These positions are President, Vice President, Secretary, and Director of Development. Please consider nominating yourself for one of these positions. Volunteers are always needed! All are welcome and encouraged to attend the meeting.
