TCYSA – COVID-19 (Coronavirus) response for Spring Season 2020

Updated – April 7, 2020

TCYSA and our member Clubs are committed to the safety of our players and their families.

In March, the TCYSA Board took the step of postponing the Spring soccer season with the hope that practices would resume the week of April 27 and games would begin on May 2.  However, with the extension of the statewide Stay Home Stay Safe order to May 4, it will not be possible to keep that schedule. In compliance with the order TCYSA will need to further revise the Spring schedule. The TCYSA Board will meet on April 19 to review the options for Spring season and share more information after that meeting. 

We wish all the best to our soccer families and hope that you are staying well and finding time to still kick a soccer ball around while maintaining social distancing.


Candice Bock
TCYSA President
