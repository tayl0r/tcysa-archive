Blackhills FC Spring Finishing and Goalkeeping Program

The Spring Finishing and Goalkeeping Program is a supplemental program for all players and coaches, regardless of their experience level. 

Players will be grouped by a combination of age, ability level, and friends/teammates.

Blackhills FC coaching staff will lead the program assisted by Blackhills FC players. 

Dates: March 8, 15, 22, 29

Cost is $45 and there is limited enrollment.
