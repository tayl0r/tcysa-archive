Last updated 2019-05-04

Grays Harbor SPLASH! Recreational Soccer Tournament

June 1st -2nd

Registration Deadline:  May 24, 2019

For teams U9 to U16

Games will be located at Aberdeen's Bishop Athletic Complex and surrounding area fields.

  • Teams will play at least 3 games, guaranteed (minimum division size will be 4 teams).
  • Teams can have 3 recreational guest players per team, up to the max roster. 
  • Players may only play on one team in the tournament.
