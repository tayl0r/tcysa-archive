Free Coaching Clinic Mar. 11th!

Last updated 2017-03-16

Saturday March 11

9:00 am - Noon

***NEW LOCATION (due to flooding):  Nisqually Middle School***

 

Instructor Kyle Jones will be offering a spring coaching clinic for our local coaches.

 

Instruction will include managing your team, structuring a successful practice, and technical topics on passing, receiving, and team play.  Come prepared to participate!

 

No registration is required.  See you on Saturday! 

 

Need more info?  Please email manager@thurstoncountysoccer.com.
