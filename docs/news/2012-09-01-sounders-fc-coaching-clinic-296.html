Last updated 2012-09-01

Sounders FC Coaching Clinic

On Thursday September 6th at CenturyLink Field from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM, coaches are invited to attend a Sounders FC training session and a half-day Next Level Coaching Clinic. Sounders FC head coach Sigi Schmid and his coaching staff will demonstrate "Next Level" coaching tips on the technical and tactical aspects of coaching while Jim Madrid of the Pacific Institute will teach the Mental Technology of Coaching. This clinic is free and you can register here!
