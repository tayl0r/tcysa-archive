Canned Food Drive BHFC

Last updated 2014-12-08

Blackhills FC Playday and Canned Food Drive

Blackhills FC will be hosting a holiday food/clothing drive next Monday and Tuesday(Nov 24th and 25th)  from 5:30 to 7pm at the Blackhills Community Soccer Complex.    We are inviting all TCYSA players aged U10-U14 to participate.  We will playing small sided games and in a pickup fashion.  No RSVP is needed.  Just show up and play. 

 

We will be collecting canned food items, Coats, Blankets and cash  to be donated to the Thurston County Food Bank and/or Community Youth Services.

 

Parents are welcome to play too.


Have a peaceful and restful family Thanksgiving!
