FIFA Changes to Laws of the Game

Last updated 2022-03-16

FIFA has made some changes and updates to the laws of the game. Our referees have been instructed to follow these changes, some of which are significant. 

There is one exception to note for TCYSA. Players are now allowed, but not required, to leave the pitch at the closest point to exit the field. 

The FIFA updates and changes can be found here, as well as in the coaches and families section of the information section at the top of this page.
