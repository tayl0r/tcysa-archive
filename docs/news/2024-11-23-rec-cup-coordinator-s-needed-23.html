Rec Cup Coordinator(s) needed!

Last updated 2024-11-23

TCYSA is looking for a Rec Cup coordinator, or a team of people who could work together to oversee the Rec Cup Tournament. We have several teams participate each year, and we are eager to find volunteers who will work with the clubs to make this yearly tournament a success.

The Rec Cup coordinator would oversee the Rec Cup Tournament, which is played over a few weekends in November and December. If you would like additional information about this position, please contact your club manager or email [email removed].
