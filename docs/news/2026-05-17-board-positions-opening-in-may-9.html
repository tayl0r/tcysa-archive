Board Positions Opening in May

Last updated 2026-05-17

TCYSA is seeking Board Members. If you would like to help TCYSA work together for youth recreational soccer in Thurston County, we invite you to join us by volunteering on our Board.

TCYSA is seeking Board members for the following positions opening in May: Vice President, Treasurer, and Secretary.

The TCYSA Board meets six times a year on the first Sunday of every other month. Elections will be held on Sunday, May 3 at 6:30 p.m. at Fire Station 31, off Franz Street in Lacey.
