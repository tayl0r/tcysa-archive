RCL Junior Academy and Player Evaluation

Last updated 2013-02-20

Washington Youth Soccer Regional Club League (RCL) Junior AcademyAcademy Program for boys and girls at the U8 and U9 age groups, children ages 6-8 can participate. The Junior Academy program is designed for recreational players that are interested in continuing to develop their technical abilities, gain game experience and enjoyment.  You can play on your recreational team and participate in this program.

We will hold an Evaluation Play Date on Monday February 4h, 6:30 – 7:30 PM at Tumwater High School Stadium. Our intention is to service as many players that are interested in the RCL Junior Academy. This will not be a tryout process. If your child wants to participate in this program and is unable to attend please email [name removed], [email removed].
