TCYSA

Last updated 2018-07-29

Thurston County Youth Soccer Association 

is comprised of six Recreational Clubs and one RCL Competitive Club.

Each Recreational Club serves its home school district, with the exception of Prairie SC which serves Yelm and Rainier School Districts. North Thurston School District is served by Chinqually Booters SC,Olympia SC and Tumwater SC. Blackhills FC, our RCL Competitive Club, serves all of Thurston County.

Please visit the logos at the bottom of the page to be directed to their respective websites.

Players sign up with Recreational Clubs to be placed on teams. TCYSA schedules the games and runs the league.
