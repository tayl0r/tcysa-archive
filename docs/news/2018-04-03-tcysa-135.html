TCYSA

Last updated 2018-04-03

Thurston County Youth Soccer Association

is comprised of five Recreational Clubs and one RCL Competitive Club

Recreational Clubs:

Olympia United SC  serves the people in the Olympia School District

Tumwater SC  serves the people in the Tumwater School District

Rochester SC  serves the people in the Rochester School District

Tenino SC serves the people in the Tenino School District

Prairie SC serves the people in the Yelm and Rainier School Districts
