TCYSA Fall Player Clinics

Last updated 2011-11-21

PLAYER CLINICS
TCYSA and Charles Paulsen,TC United's trainer will host Fall player Clinics on Friday nights at the Regional Athletic Complex  5:30 pm to 7:00 pm.   Field #3


U8 to U9 players will meet on Sept. 9th, Sept. 30th and October 21st.

U9 to U10 players will meet September 16th, October 7th and October 28th.

U10 and older players will meet September 23nd, October 14th and November 4th.

3 sessions for $10.00
20 max per session
Registration opens online August 8th.
