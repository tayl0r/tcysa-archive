Last updated 2015-05-18

TCYSA Annual General Meeting

May 17 at 6:30 pm

Thurston County Courthouse Building 1

 

The Board will hold elections per TCYSA Bylaws at the AGM in May.  The following positions are open: President, Secretary, Treasurer, Director of  Competition, Director of Development, and Registrar. Please consider nominating yourself for a position.  TCYSA holds 6 meeting per year and most positions should not consume a large amount of additional time for volunteers.  A ballot vote will be held at the AGM.  Please email me if you would like additional information or would like to nominate yourself or someone else for a position. 
 
Carla Borgaard
OUSC Club Manager
