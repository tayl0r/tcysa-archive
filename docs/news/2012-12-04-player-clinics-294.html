Player Clinics
Thurston County United, in partnership with Saint Martin’s University’s Men’s & Women’s Soccer Program are beginning a partnership with one major objective: to create a development program that improves the playing and coaching experience for TCU players and coaches.  The program will be led by SMU coaches: Rob Walker, Bre Giove and Dave Cross with assistance from TCU Staff and SMU players.
October  Academy- Sunday, October 7th,14th,21th,28th  from 5pm-7pm at RAC Field #1
Cost: $40
