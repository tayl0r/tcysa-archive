Spring Player Skills Clinics

Last updated 2011-04-03

Spring Player Skills Clinics on Fridays

See the top tool Bar to Enroll "Skills Clinic Registration"

Group 1: players U8 and U9 meet March 4th, April 8th and May 6th

Group 2: players U9, U10 and U11 meet March 11th, April 15th and May 13th

Group 3: players age U11, U12 and Older meet March 18th, April 29th and May 20th

All Sessions 5:30 pm to 7:00 pm at Regional Athletic Complex in Lacey

Registration open now until March 4th

Cost is $10.00 for the 3 sessions   Limit 45 per group

TCYSA will group the youngest 45 registrants into Group 1, mid ages next 45 into Group 2 and the oldest 45 into Group 3.

Everyone will receive an e-mail as  to which Group they are in.
