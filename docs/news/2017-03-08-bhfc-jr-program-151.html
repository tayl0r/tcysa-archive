BHFC Jr Program

Last updated 2017-03-08

Blackhills FC will be holding a Junior Program for boys and girls ages 6-9.

Designed for players who want  

  • to develop their technical abilities,
  •  to gain game experience, and
  • to have fun

Players can be rostered to recreation teams and participate.

Ages: 6 - 9 year old boys and girls

Dates: February 10th, 24th and March 3rd, 10th, 17th, 24th, 31st

Training Day: Fridays 6:00 - 7:00 PM at the Capital Soccer Fields (2832 SW 93rd Ave, Tumwater WA)

Game Days: 4 in-house game days on Sundays – 3/5, 3/12, 3/19, and 3/26 at the Capital Soccer Fields-

Cost: $50 (includes jersey)

Registration Deadline: February 3rd, 2017...See our brochure for more details.
