Summer Friday Night Player Clinics with Charles Paulsen.

Group 1: U8 to U10
June 10th, July 15th, and August 5th
Group 2:  U11 to U13
June 17th, July 22nd, August 12th

June 24th, July 29th, August 19th

5:30 pm to 7:00 pm
