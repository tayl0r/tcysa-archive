New Referee Clinic

Last updated 2021-06-25

Interested in becoming a referee? There is a New Referee Clinic scheduled for July 31 at Stan Hedwall Park in Chehalis. You can find all the details for this clinic at the link below. 

http://www.wareferees.org/

  • To register for the clinic, go to the state website, http://www.wareferees.org
  • Under the banner, select Instruction> Clinic & Events Schedule
  • Select: Entry, Never Been a USSF Referee Before
  • Scroll down to the last clinic listed, SRC, New Referee,(GR, Online PreReq) 08-00-0622-PR8, Select “Register”
  • Complete Personal Information Page including User Name and Password.

At this point you are registered to begin work on the Online Lessons, which need to be completed. When you have completed the lessons, you may then transfer to the Chehalis clinic, or to any clinic you want. The most important thing, please complete the online lessons as soon as possible, thus guaranteeing your transfer to your local Chehalis clinic.
