National "E" Coaching Clinic

Washington State Youth Soccer is offering a National “E” license course here in Olympia the weekend of May 6-8.  The “E” License targets coaches working with children 9 – 12 years of age and is a prerequisite for the “D” License.  For those who are interested, you may register, and obtain more information, at the following link

http://www.washingtonyouthsoccer.org/coaches/coaching_education/course_schedule/
