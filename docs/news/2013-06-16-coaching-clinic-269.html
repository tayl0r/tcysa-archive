Coaching clinic

Last updated 2013-06-16

Free Coach Training with Black Hills Trainer David Cross
 
This coach training is focused on recreation coaches and is open to all TCYSA coaches and assistant coaches.  We encourage all coaches to participate whether you are a new coach or a returning coach who would like to build on the training you received prior to the spring season.
 
Saturday,  June 8th
Rainier Vista Park
U8 - U10 Coaches:    9:30 - 12:00        
U11 - U18 Coaches:  1:00 - 3:30           
 
Coaches should wear cleats, bring a water bottle, and be prepared to participate!
Please RSVP before June 6th to your club's manager if you plan to attend
