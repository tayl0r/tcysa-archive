TCYSA – COVID-19 (Coronavirus) response for Spring Season 2020

Updated – April 19, 2020

TCYSA and our member Clubs are committed to the safety of our players and their families.

The TCYSA Board has determined that it is necessary to fully cancel the Spring 2020 youth recreation soccer season. We are all disappointed to have to take this step, but safety and public health are the highest priorities.

While there are still many unknowns about when the Stay Home Stay Safe order will be lifted and when fields will reopen, the Board felt this action was in the best interest of all our Clubs and soccer players and their families.

The TCYSA Board will meet again on May 17 to make further determinations about summer play and the Fall season.

We encourage everyone to continue to do their part to Stay Home and Stay Safe and practice social distancing. We also encourage families to find safe ways to keep those soccer skills sharp and have fun while getting in some physical activity. Most Clubs are able to provide soccer balls to families that need them and there are many sources for online for drill and skill building exercises.
