Last updated 2018-06-27

In memory of Jani Morrish

TCYSA would like to express our sadness at the passing of Jani Morrish after a courageous battle with cancer.  Jani was a true champion for youth soccer.  She was the long-time manager of the Prairie Soccer Club in Yelm.  She and her family have given countless hours to youth soccer in Yelm and around Thurston County. We will always be grateful to Jani for her leadership and selflessness. Our thoughts and prayers are with Jani’s family and friends.
