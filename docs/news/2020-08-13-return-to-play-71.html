Return to Play

Last updated 2020-08-13

With Summer Season approaching quickly, please read the TCYSA Return to Play guidelines. There is currently no requirement for players to wear masks during training, practice, games, or while exercising. However, masks should be worn before and after all listed activities. Referees are not required to wear masks during games. Spectators should wear masks at all times.

For more information, please read the Governor's Return to Play for Professional and Recreational Sports.
