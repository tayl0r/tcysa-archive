Updated Safety Guidelines

Last updated 2021-04-15

Thank you for a great start to the Spring recreational soccer season. We had really strong compliance with safety requirements during our first weekend of games. This week Thurston County moved into Phase 3 which means a small change in mask requirements - players are able to remove their masks while actively playing during a game. Players must wear masks at all other times and there are no changes to the requirements that all coaches and volunteers must wear masks at all times. Spectators must also wear masks at all times per State requirements. Please help us have a safe and enjoyable season by following all TCYSA safety policies and any local requirements. We will continue to provide notice if there are any further updates to these safety requirements.

Candice Bock

TCYSA President
