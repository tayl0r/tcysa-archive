U11 teams NO slide tackling

Last updated 2015-10-25

TCYSA League U11 Teams

 

Staring Summer 2015 and going forward,  U11 teams will not be allowed to Slide Tackle in the TCYSA Recreational Leagues.

Safety is a primary concern for TCYSA and the Board voted this rule back in place at the May AGM.

U11 teams, if you enter tournaments  (including the Recreational Cup) outside of TCYSA, slide tackling is allowed by WSYS rules.
