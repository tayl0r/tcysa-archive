Blackhills Summer Camps

Last updated 2021-08-03

Blackhills Football Club has many programs and camps to offer the community. The information for Summer offerings is below and you can find more information on upcoming Fall programs by looking under the Camps & Programs tab at the top of this page.

 

SUMMER CAMPS 
Activities to develop and support goal scoring, creating chances and playing small sided games.  Sessions to include high shooting repetitions and 1v1 – 4v4 small sided-games.
 Dates:  Camp #1 July 19-20-21 (9:00 – 12:00 PM) / Camp #2 August 16-17-18 (9:00 – 12:00 PM Who:  ages 8 - 14 Location:  Capital Soccer Fields, Tumwater Cost:  $70 (each camp) Registration:  [email removed]
 
 TOPSOCCER 
TOPSoccer is for special needs kids and adults that want to experience the sport of soccer. Players will participate in various activities such as movements and basics of the game in a fun environment, guided by Blackhills FC coaches and players. Parents are welcome to join their child on the field or they can join in with one of our TOPSoccer Buddies.
 Dates:  August 6, 13, 20, 27 6:00 – 7:00 PM Who:  All children 4+ with special needs Location:  Capital Soccer Fields, Tumwater Cost:  FREE Registration:  [email removed]
 
2v2 PLAYBALL:  
Dribbling and Scoring goals through game play Blackhills FC is excited to bring the playground to the field with 2v2 soccer.  We will be using the Belgium 2v2 training model, dribbling football and scoring goals!  PLAYBALL instruction will be taught by Blackhills FC coaches and players.  
 Dates:  August 3, 10, 17, 24 Who: ages 5 - 9 Location:  Capital Soccer Fields, Tumwater Cost:  $25 Registration:  [email removed]
