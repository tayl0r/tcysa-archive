Directions to Blackhills Community Soccer Complex

Blackhills Community Soccer Complex:
Take exit #99 from I-5. 
    For the North Bound Go left or West across the top of freeway to the light and then 1500' more to Guardian Self Storage sign on your right. We share their Driveway.  Travel 1500' North to The Complex   
    For the South Bound go to light and turn right or West. Travel 1500' to Guardian Self Storage on your right. We share their Driveway. Travel 1500' North to The Complex.
    Some Nav System as well as Google are at times incorrect.
