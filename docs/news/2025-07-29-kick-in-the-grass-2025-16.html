Kick in the Grass 2025

Last updated 2025-07-29

 It's almost time for the 46th annual Kick in the Grass tournament! Sign up now to be part of the longest-running soccer tournament in Washington.

When: July 18-20, 2025. Register by July 4th.

Who: Boys and Girls teams U10-U10. Brackets open to all Recreational, Select, Premier and US Club teams.

Where: Tumwater, WA
