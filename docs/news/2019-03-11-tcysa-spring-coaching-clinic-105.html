TCYSA Spring Coaching Clinic

Last updated 2019-03-11

TCYSA Spring Coaching Clinic - Free and Open to all TCYSA Coaches

TCYSA looks forward to providing coaching support through the TCYSA Spring Coaching Clinic. This opportunity will give new coaches confidence to go into their season, and experienced coaches new information on designing and delivering a training session. Coaches may attend one or both sessions. Clinics are free and open to all coaches and volunteers.

What: TCYSA Spring Mod Coaching Clinic

When: Saturday, March 16th

Level 1 (beginner coach) 9:00-10:30

Level 2 (experienced coach) 10:30-12:00

Where: Capital Soccer Fields

What to Bring: notebook and pen, water, snack, weather appropriate clothing, cleats or athletic shoes so you can participate in field sessions.

Players Needed: We want to have as many players as we can for demonstration purposes. Please invite players that you know!

*There is a minimum number of registered participants needed for the course.


To register or if you have questions, please email Kyle at development@thurstoncountysoccer.com. Please include your name, age group, what clinic you will be attending, and your TCYSA Club.
