Silent Saturday

May 2, 2015

 

"Silent Saturday" is ONE DAY out of the schedule in which parents, coaches and spectators are encouraged not to talk to or call out to players on the field during the game. This may sound like a radical idea, but the program has met with huge success in both large and small clubs throughout the United States.
