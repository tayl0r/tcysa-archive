New Referee Clinic

Last updated 2019-09-06

New Referee Clinic

Saturday, August 10th, 9:00a-2:00p

Location: Pioneer Park Field #1

Registration will be open on Wednesday, July 10.

There are limited slots available so be sure to register as early as possible!

 The link to access New Referee Clinic information on the WA State Referee Committee homepage is:

 http://www.wareferees.org

 At the top of the page select: Instruction > Clinic/ Events Schedule

On the next page in the “Please Select” drop down menu: Become a Referee: Never been a USSF Referee before.

On the next page select the Tumwater clinic of 8/10 and Register with WARefees.

Follow the Registration Instructions and pay the USSF fee and you are registered for the clinic.
