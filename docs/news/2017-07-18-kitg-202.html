KICK IN THE GRASS

38th Annual Kick in the Grass Tournament
July 14, 15, & 16~ 2017
The KITG tournament is hosted by the Tumwater Soccer Club. Teams from all over SW Washington and Northern Oregon have participated in KITG events.

It is the goal of the Tumwater Soccer Club for the KITG to provide an enjoyable, first class soccer experience to all its participants - players, parents, coaches, and referees. The KITG provides a first rate soccer experience in a small community atmosphere.

It is the view of the Tumwater Soccer Club that above all else, we should maintain a respect for our competition, our players, and for the game. Furthermore, we believe that exemplary sportmanship should be rewarded. To that end, Sportmanship Trophies are awarded to one team in each of the following groups: Girls U10 thru U14, Girls U15 thu U19, Boys U10 thru U14, and Boys U15 thru U19.

Recreational, Select, Premier, RCL, & US Club Teams Welcome
