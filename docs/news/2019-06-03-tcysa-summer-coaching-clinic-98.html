Last updated 2019-06-03

TCYSA Summer Coaching Clinic

Free and Open to all TCYSA Coaches

This opportunity will give new coaches confidence to go into their season, and experienced coaches new information on designing and delivering a training session. Coaches may attend one or both sessions. Clinics are free and open to all coaches and volunteers.

Please email Kyle Jones at   development@thurstoncountysoccer.com   to register. Be sure to include your name, age group you are coaching, what clinic you will be attending, and your TCYSA Club.

When: Saturday, June 8th

Level 1 (beginner coach) 9:00-10:30

Level 2 (experienced coach) 10:30-12:00

Where: Capital Soccer Fields

What to Bring: notebook and pen, water, snack, weather appropriate clothing, cleats or athletic shoes if you can participate in field sessions.

Players Needed: We want to have as many players as we can for demonstration purposes. Please invite players that you know!
