MLS Tournament
Mod teams U8, U9 and U10
June 3,4,5, 2011

With so many mod teams playing this spring, we thought it would be fun to give teams the opportunity to play some of the teams they did not play in the regular season and the chance to be division winners complete with medals for first and second place teams and pins for all participants.
Games will be played in Lacey, mostly at Rainier Vista and U8 at Thornbury.  Schedules will be posted on this website, with scores and standings posted online as games finish.
Cost is $150.00 per team, 3 game minimum.
To register your team, click on MLS Tournament Registration tab top tool bar.
Teams for tournament are same age as for spring play.
Players can include those from either spring or summer rosters 2011
as long as they are appropriate age/gender for your team and properly registered with WSYSA. See MLS Rules in download section.
