Coronavirus (COVID-19) information

Last updated 2020-04-07

CORONAVIRUS (COVID-19)

TCYSA's Board has made the decision to postpone the Spring 2020 season. At this point, our season will start May 2 and continue until June 20. TCYSA's goal is to play a seven game season, with the understanding that this may change. For more information, please read the full response from the Board.

 

Washington Youth Soccer is following the guidelines set by the Center for Disease Control (CDC) and Washington State Department of Health.  You can find more information, along with additional hyperlinks for references, HERE.
