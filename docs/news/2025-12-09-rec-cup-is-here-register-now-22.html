Rec Cup is here - Register NOW

Last updated 2025-12-09

 

Rec Cup is here - Register with your club NOW!

Who - Fall rec teams U9 and older

When/Where for U9/U10 & U11/U12 - November 1 & 2 (District games) at Capital Soccer Fields. (Group play and quarter-finals) 

When/Where for U13 and older (Full Field) - Nov 1 & 2 (Group play). December 7 & 8 (Quarter-finals) at the Regional Athletic Complex (the RAC). December 13 & 14 (Semi-finals and Championship) at Starfire in Tukwila. 

Cost - $400 per team, payable to your club

Registration is open now and will close OCTOBER 19. Please contact your club manager or registrar to register.

If there are any questions regarding the Rec Cup, please reach out to our district 7 Rec Cup Coordinator, Joe Beaulieu, at reccup@thurstoncountysoccer.com.
