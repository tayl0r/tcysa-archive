TOPSoccer program for players with special needs

TOPSoccer is for special needs kids and adults that want to experience the sport of soccer. This program is free and a great place for players of all abilities!

Who: All players with special needs

What: Blackhills FC TOPSoccer Program

When: Tuesdays August 6th, 13th, 20th and 27th from 6:00 – 7:00 PM

Where: Capital Soccer Fields (2832 93rd Ave SW, 98512)

Cost: Free
