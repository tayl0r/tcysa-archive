Sounders Game U23

Last updated 2016-05-12

Sounders U23 Game

June 5th,  South Sound Stadium  at 2 pm

The Sounders U23 team is coming back to Thurston County to face the Portland Timbers U23 team.  We watched current Sounders forward Jordan Morris play in the U23 match two years ago, and Deandre Yedlin play in the U23 match four years ago.  Come see some top quality soccer at bargain prices($10 per ticket). Also, the price of the pregame clinic has been reduced to $25(which includes a ticket to the match).  The clinic is sure to sell out, so grab a spot while available.   

We will have lots of fun events at the match(giveaways, raffles, halftime entertainment, autographs, etc).
