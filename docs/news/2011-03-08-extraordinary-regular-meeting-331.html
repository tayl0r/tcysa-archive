Extraordinary Regular Meeting

Last updated 2011-03-08

Thurston County Youth Soccer Association has scheduled an Extraordinary General Meeting to consider the adoption of revised Association Bylaws.  The proposed new Bylaws, which are available on this website here, have been considered and approved by the Boards  of the Association's member  clubs: Chinqually Booters Soccer Club and Prairie Soccer Club. The new Bylaws will facilitate other clubs in Thurston County (Olympia United Youth Soccer Club,  Tumwater Soccer Club and Black Hills Football Club) joining our Association.  Our Goal is to provide a better soccer experience to the 4,000 youth players and their families in our area.
All are welcome to attend. Each Chinqually and Prairie team is entitled to nominate a representative to vote at the EGM

Sunday,  March 6th, 2011  Lacey Fire Station #31
located at 1231 Franz Street, Lacey    Time  6:30 pm
