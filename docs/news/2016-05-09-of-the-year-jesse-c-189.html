BHFC's Jesse Costello

Region IV Coach of the Year for Competitive Girls' Soccer

Jesse Costello has been invited to attend the US Youth Soccer Awards Gala in Baltimore, Maryland early next year. Region IV comprises 13 western states and includes some of the most successful clubs in US youth soccer.
