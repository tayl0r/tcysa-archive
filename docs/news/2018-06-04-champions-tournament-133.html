Champions Tournament

June 1-3, 2018

Primary location at the Regional Athletic Complex (RAC) in Lacey  

 Open to U11– U19 Recreational Teams currently registered in Washington Youth Soccer, and other state associations affiliated with USYS and US Club Soccer

Boys & Girls Divisions. Each team is guaranteed 3 games & all games will consist of two 30 minute halves. First place teams in each Division will receive a token of recognition

Registration deadline April 25, 2018.  Late registration subject to $50.00 late fee.
