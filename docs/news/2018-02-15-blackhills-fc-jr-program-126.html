Blackhills FC Jr. Program

Last updated 2018-02-15

 
       BLACKHILLS FC JR. PROGRAM

Blackhills FC will be holding a Junior Program for boys and girls ages 6-9. The Junior Program is designed for players that are interested in continuing to develop their soccer abilities and have fun playing soccer.
Players can be rostered with recreational teams and participate. 

Our intention is to service as many players that are interested in the Junior Program.

Blackhills FC will form a training pool of players.  This allows free movement of players during training and games as they are not fixed to specific teams, creating the opportunity for more “like vs like” training and game play.  
Ages: 6 - 9 year old boys and girls 
Dates: March 2nd, 9th, 16th, 23rd, 30th and April 6th  
Training Day: Fridays 6:00 - 7:00 PM at the Capital Soccer Fields 
Training Location: Capital Soccer Fields, 2832 SW 93rd Ave, Tumwater WA 
Game Days: 4 in house game days on Sundays March 11th, 18th, 25th and April 8th at Capital Soccer Fields from 2:00 – 3:00
Cost: $50 (includes t-shirt)
Registration Deadline: Friday, February 23rd 
Registration: https://blackhillsfc.demosphere-secure.com/_registration
Program: 
• 7v7 and 5v5 Format (includes GK). 6 year olds will play 4v4 without a goalkeeper
• Player centered environment 
• Licensed coaches to work with the players 
• US Soccer designed curriculum 
• Set coaches for each age group
