BLACKHILLS FC UNDER THE LIGHTS

Free Community Game Nights!

The "Under the Lights" program will focus on individual soccer techniques, 1v1 attacking, body coordination, and enjoyment through small sided games. We will have goalkeeper training available at the "Under the Lights" program.

The Blackhills FC Coaching Staff will lead the instruction and Blackhills FC Players will assist in the development of these young soccer players.

All Coaches, regardless of their experience level, are welcome to participate for Coaches Education. Please contact Kyle Jones at: [email removed] if interested.

Children will be grouped by a combination of age, ability level and friend(s)/teammates. No experience necessary, a perfect place for beginners!

Dates: January 8th, 22nd, 29th and February 5th Location: Tumwater High School Age and Time: Ages 6-9, 5:30 - 6:30 PM Age and Time: Ages 10 -13, 6:45- 8:00 PM Goalkeepers: Ages 10 - 16, 5:30 - 6:30

Registration is free this year! Spaces limited to 70 6-9 year olds, 70 10-13 year olds, and 20 goalkeepers.

Program Registration: https://www.secure-sam.com/www.blackhillsfc.org/sam/registration

Program Questions: Please contact Kyle Jones - [email removed]
