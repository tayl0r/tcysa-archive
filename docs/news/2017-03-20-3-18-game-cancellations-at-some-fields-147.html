3/18 Game Cancellations at some fields!!

Last updated 2017-03-20

All home games on Tumwater School District fields are canceled for 3/18.
All Prairie Soccer Club home games at Longmire, Yelm MS, or Yelm HS are canceled for 3/18.
All Capital Soccer Fields are CLOSED this weekend, too.

Affected team coaches will be informed via email, and online schedules will be updated tonight.

Fields affected are

Black Hills HS #1
Capital Soccer Fields (all fields)
East Olympia Elementary field
Michael T. Simmons Elementary field
Tumwater High School #1, #2
Longmire Park - all fields
Yelm Middle School
Yelm High School

Games will be rescheduled to the end of the season.  In some cases, games will have to occur on the Memorial Day weekend (5/27).  If a team wishes to opt out of their Memorial Day weekend rescheduled game, its coach should email manager@thurstoncountysoccer.com once the new game appears on the schedule.
