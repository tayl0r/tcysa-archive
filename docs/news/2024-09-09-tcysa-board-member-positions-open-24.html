TCYSA Board Member positions open

Last updated 2024-09-09

TCYSA is seeking Board Members. If you would like to help TCYSA work together for youth recreational soccer in Thurston County, we would invite you to join us by volunteering on our Board.

TCYSA is seeking Board members for the following positions: Vice President, Treasurer, and Secretary. We are also seeking a Rec Cup coordinator.

The TCYSA Board meets six times a year on the first Sunday of every other month. Elections will be held on Sunday, September 8 at 6:30 p.m.at Fire Station 31, off Franz Street in Lacey. If interested in volunteering for these Board positions or would like additional information, please contact Carla Borgaard at [email removed].

The Rec Cup coordinator would oversee the Rec Cup Tournament, which is played over a few weekends in November and December. If you would like additional information about this position, please contact your club manager or email [email removed].
