TCYSA Board Member positions open

Last updated 2025-05-10

TCYSA is seeking organized, motivated volunteers to serve in board positions for a two-year term. Soccer experience is helpful. The board meets bi-monthly for about 2.5 hours. Board members should also be available to read and respond to emails regarding board business as necessary. If you would like to nominate yourself or someone else for any of the positions below, or would just like more information, please contact secretary@thurstoncountysoccer.com.  


The following board positions are up for election:

President
Director of Competition

In addition, the position of Secretary is currently vacant, and we will be seeking to fill it for the remainder of the unexpired term (May 2026).

Please reach out to secretary@thurstoncountysoccer.com if you have questions or would like more information.
