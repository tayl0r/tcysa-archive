BHFC Tryouts

Last updated 2016-05-12

We are the premier soccer club of the Thurston County Youth Soccer Association, with premier and/or select teams for each age group and gender. Our nationally licensed coaches are dedicated to developing high quality youth soccer in the Thurston County Community, while providing the resources necessary for each player to develop his or her soccer skills at the most appropriate pace. BlackHills Football Club is one of the top clubs in the state that participates in the Washington Youth Soccer RCL League, which provides the highest level league play and player development. We are the only Club in Thurston County with players in Washington Youth Soccer Elite Player Development program, which participates in U.S. Youth Soccer Olympic Player Development.
