Sounders Mens U23 Game, June 4th, 2 PM, NTHS!

Last updated 2017-06-06

We are pleased to announce that Blackhills FC will again host Sounders Mens U23 vs. Portland Timbers Mens U23 on Sunday June 4th,  2:00 PM kick off at North Thurston High School.  In addition the Sounders U23 team will provide a pre-match clinic for boys and girls aged 5 - 15, 12:00 - 1:00 PM (11:45 AM Check-in).  Pre-match clinic is $25 per child and also includes a match ticket.
