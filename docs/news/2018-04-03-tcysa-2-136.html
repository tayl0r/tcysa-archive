Last updated 2018-04-03

Thurston County Youth Soccer Association

is comprised of five Recreational Clubs and one RCL Competitive Club

Competitive Club:

Blackhills FC (BFC) is the RCL Club for TCYSA and serves all of Thurston County

See the club logos at the bottom of the page to be directed to their respective websites.

Players sign up with clubs to be placed on teams.  TCYSA schedules the games and runs the leagues.
