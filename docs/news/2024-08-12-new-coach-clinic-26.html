New Coach Clinic

Last updated 2024-08-12

New coach? Need some training to start the season? Come join the Director of Coaching for Chinqually Booters Soccer Club for a pre-season new coach training! Kyle Jones will help you get ready for your first practice and your first game, and will be available to answer questions you may have. He has a lot of coaching experience - you don't want to miss this! Geared for new coaches, this is open to all coaches, regardless of experience.

August 13, 2024

Rainier Vista

6:00 to 7:30
