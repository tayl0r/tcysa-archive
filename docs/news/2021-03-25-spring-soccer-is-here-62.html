Spring Soccer is here!

Last updated 2021-03-25

Spring recreational soccer games start up this weekend (March 20) and we are very excited for the kids to be back out on the pitch. Safety is still our priority and we are following current State phase 2 requirements that all players, coaches and volunteers must wear masks at all times and spectators must wear masks or physically distance. Additionally, for all games held at City of Lacey Parks' fields all spectators are required to wear masks at all times per the City's safety requirements. Field maps for all City of Lacey fields in use by TCYSA this season can be found at the top of our page.  Please help us have a safe and enjoyable season by following all TCYSA safety policies and any local requirements.
