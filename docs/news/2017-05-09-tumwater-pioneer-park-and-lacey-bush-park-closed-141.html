Tumwater Pioneer Park and Lacey Bush Park closed.

Last updated 2017-05-09

UPDATED INFO!!! (as of 5/5)

Due to the thunderstorm yesterday, Tumwater Pioneer Park and Lacey's Bush Park are closed for May 6-7.

All full-sided games have been relocated to Chinook MS fields.  Check your team schedules for exact info.

The two U12 games at Tumwater Pioneer Park and the three games at Bush Park have been relocated to Capital Soccer Fields.

 

Thank you, coaches and parents, for being patient with what has certainly been a wet spring season so far!
