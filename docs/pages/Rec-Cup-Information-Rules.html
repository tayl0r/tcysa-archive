Rec Cup Information & Rules

The Washington Youth Soccer (WYS) Rec Cup is a fun, end-of-season tournament hosted by Washington Youth Soccer for recreational teams across the state. It gives players a chance to compete in a festive, championship-style environment while celebrating the growth and skills they've developed during the season. The focus is on sportsmanship, community, and a memorable soccer experience for all participants. 


Washington Youth Soccer, together with District 7 (TCYSA, GHFYSA, and LCYSA) brings you the Rec Cup 2026! 

Rec Cup dates are as follows:

U9 - U12 (Boys and Girls) - November 7 &8.

Capital Soccer Fields.

U13 and older - November 7 & 8 for group play and some quarterfinals, depending on group size. Larger age groups will play their quarter finals on December 5 & 6 at the Regional Athletic Complex (the RAC). 

         

For general Rec Cup information, please visit the Washington Youth Soccer website.
