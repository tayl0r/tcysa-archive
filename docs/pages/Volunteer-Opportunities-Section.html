Volunteer - Opportunities Section

TCYSA has a Board that provided governance for our affiliated clubs and runs the leagues for those clubs to participate in.

TCYSA Board is always looking for volunteers that are committed to youth soccer and interested in improving the game both on and off the field.  Each position on the Board is a two-year commitment.

If you are interested in volunteering contact Sara at manager@thurstoncountysoccer.com.
