League Info

TCYSA administers three seasons of Recreational Soccer each year.

Seasons are normally eight games and are dependent on field availability and the calendar.

Spring Season games typically begin on the third Saturday in March and end the Saturday before Memorial Day.

 

Summer Season generally begins the third Monday in June with games being played Monday through Thursday.  Girls play Mondays and Wednesdays and Boys play Tuesdays and Thursdays. Game times are 6:00 and 7:30 pm.  We also offer High School Co-ed in the summer. The season ends July 31.

 

Fall Season generally starts on the first Saturday after Labor Day and runs through the last weekend in October.
