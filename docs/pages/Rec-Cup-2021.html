WYS RECREATIONAL CUP 2021

The District 7 Preliminary Tournament is your entry point into the 2021 WYS Recreational Cup.

The champions and wildcards from each District in WYS then proceed to the quarterfinals.

This tournament is for Age Groups U11 and Up.
