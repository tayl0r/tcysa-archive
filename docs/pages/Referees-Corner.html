Referees Corner

"New Referee Clinics" are offered throughout the year

by the Washington State Referee Committee (WASRC).

Go to www.wareferees.org to find a clinic and register online.

Need instructions on how to register?  Click here.

 

Step 1 - Register with the WASRC Website and then Sign Up for the Online Pre-Clinic

  • The registration fee is $85.00 (USSF registration fee $65+$20 Clinic Fee) to be paid online during the registration process.
  • If you are 18+ years old, there is an addition $3.50 fee to cover a background check.

Step 2 - Complete the Pre-Clinic Lessons. You must study online and take the online test before attending the In-Person Clinic.

Step 3 - Choose an In-Person Clinic

There are entry level clinics throughout the year.
